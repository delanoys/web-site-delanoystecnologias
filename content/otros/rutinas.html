Daily Routines - Rutinas Diarias

💡 Explicación: El Grupo VIP (Tercera Persona)

Cuando hablamos de Nicol, Dilan u Obeth (He/She), los verbos cambian un poquito. Es como si entraran a un club VIP donde se les regala una "s" o "es" al final del verbo.

  • Wake up cambia a wakes up
  • Get up cambia a gets up
  • Brush cambia a brushes
  • Go cambia a goes
  • Study cambia a studies

Nicol's Routine SHE (Ella)

Wake up: Nicol wakes up early every morning. Nicol se despierta temprano cada mañana.
Get up: Nicol gets up at 6:00 a.m. Nicol se levanta a las 6:00 a.m.
Take a shower: Nicol takes a shower before school. Nicol se ducha antes de ir a la escuela.
Brush teeth: Nicol brushes her teeth after breakfast. Nicol se cepilla los dientes después del desayuno.
Get dressed: Nicol gets dressed quickly. Nicol se viste rápido.
Have breakfast: Nicol has breakfast with her family. Nicol desayuna con su familia.
Go to school: Nicol goes to school at 7:00 a.m. Nicol va a la escuela a las 7:00 a.m.
Study: Nicol studies English in class. Nicol estudia inglés en clase.

Dilan's Routine HE (Él)

Wake up: Dilan wakes up at 7:00 a.m. Dilan se despierta a las 7:00 a.m.
Get up: Dilan gets up quickly. Dilan se levanta rápido.
Take a shower: Dilan takes a shower every day. Dilan se ducha todos los días.
Brush teeth: Dilan brushes his teeth in the morning. Dilan se cepilla los dientes en la mañana.
Get dressed: Dilan gets dressed for school. Dilan se viste para la escuela.
Have breakfast: Dilan has breakfast at home. Dilan desayuna en casa.
Go to school: Dilan goes to school by bus. Dilan va a la escuela en bus.
Read: Dilan reads a book in class. Dilan lee un libro en clase.

Obeth's Routine HE (Él)

Wake up: Obeth wakes up early. Obeth se despierta temprano.
Get up: Obeth gets up at 6:30 a.m. Obeth se levanta a las 6:30 a.m.
Take a shower: Obeth takes a shower before breakfast. Obeth se ducha antes del desayuno.
Brush teeth: Obeth brushes his teeth after eating. Obeth se cepilla los dientes después de comer.
Get dressed: Obeth gets dressed every morning. Obeth se viste cada mañana.
Have breakfast: Obeth has breakfast with his family. Obeth desayuna con su familia.
Go to school: Obeth goes to school on time. Obeth va a la escuela a tiempo.
Play: Obeth plays with his friends after school. Obeth juega con sus amigos después de la escuela.
